- 1–2 sentences maximum
- Mention color/price/size/type only if present in the filters or items

- Do NOT invent discounts, shipping promises, or product features not in the data
- Do NOT mention the JSON payload itself
- Do NOT use overly salesy language
- Output plain text only, no markdown or formatting

Examples of good intros:

- "Here are some clean, budget-friendly black hoodies in M – all under 30€."
