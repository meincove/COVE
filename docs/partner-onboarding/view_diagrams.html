🎨 Flow 1: Small Solo Founder (Manual Onboarding)

This flow is designed for independent designers and small brands who manually enter their products. It includes both UI form entry and CSV bulk upload options.

graph TD A[Brand Discovers COVE] --> B[Clicks 'Become a Partner'] B --> C[Registration Form] C --> D{Account Creation} D --> E[Email: Brand Name, Contact Info] E --> F[Business: VAT, Country, Address] F --> G[Shipping: Policies, Processing Time] G --> H[Payment: Stripe Connect Onboarding] H --> I[Dashboard Access] I --> J{Add Products} J --> K1[Option 1: Manual UI Form] J --> K2[Option 2: CSV Upload] K1 --> L[Fill Product Details] L --> M[Add Color Variants] M --> N[Upload Images] N --> O[Set Sizes & Stock] O --> P[Preview Product] K2 --> Q[Download CSV Template] Q --> R[Fill Template Offline] R --> S[Upload CSV File] S --> T[AI Validation & Mapping] T --> U{Valid?} U -->|No| V[Show Errors, Fix CSV] V --> S U -->|Yes| W[Import Products] P --> X[Submit for Review] W --> X X --> Y{COVE Admin Review} Y -->|Approved| Z[Brand Goes Live] Y -->|Rejected| AA[Feedback Sent, Fix Issues] AA --> J Z --> AB[Products Visible on COVE]

🔗 Flow 3: Affiliate Brand (Feed Integration)

Fully automated integration with affiliate networks (Awin, ShareASale, CJ Affiliate). COVE automatically syncs product catalogs daily with AI enrichment.

🔧 Data Ingestion & Normalization Pipeline

✅ Key Takeaways

  • 3 Onboarding Paths: Manual, API Integration, and Affiliate Feeds
  • AI-Powered: Automatic validation, enrichment, and quality control
