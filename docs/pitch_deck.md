# Slide 1: The Problem
## The "Query Gap" in Online Fashion

*   **Users know what they want:** *"A complete outfit for a summer wedding in Tuscany under €300."*
*   **Search bars only understand keywords:** *"Blue dress", "Sandals".*
*   **Result:** Endless browsing, sizing uncertainty, and high return rates.
*   **Traditional Chatbots:** Reactive, scripted, and frustrating.

# Slide 2: The Solution
## Meet Cove: The First AI-Native Fashion Platform

**We don't just search. We think, plan, and act.**

*   **Cove Vision:** A privacy-first, config-driven intelligence layer.
*   **Outcome:** Users get complete solutions (outfits, bundles), not just search results.

# Slide 4: The "Global Vision"
## Virtual Try-On (VTO) & Reducing Returns

*   **The Pain Point:** High return rates due to "will it fit?" anxiety.
*   **The Innovation:** proprietary **IDM-VTO** (Generative AI) integration.
*   **The Experience:**
    1.  User uploads photo.
    2.  Selects garment.
    3.  **Visualizes** fit and drape instantly.
*   **Impact:** Drastically reduced decision fatigue and higher conversion.
